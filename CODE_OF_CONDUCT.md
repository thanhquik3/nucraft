# Code of Conduct / Quy Tắc Cộng Đồng

This repository is a personal, non-commercial hobby open-source project hosted on GitHub. All contributors, users, and maintainers agree to abide by the [GitHub Community Guidelines](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-community-guidelines) and [GitHub Acceptable Use Policies](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/github-acceptable-use-policies).

## Our Standards
- Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming in issues, discussions, and pull requests.
- Do not post spam, harassment, hate speech, personal private information (doxxing), malicious payloads, or automated abuse.
- Keep contributions relevant to the NUCRAFT voxel sandbox game and properly licensed under MIT.

## Scope & Enforcement
The project maintainer (`thanhquik3`) reserves the right to remove spam, abusive comments, or off-topic content that violates these guidelines or GitHub's platform rules.
